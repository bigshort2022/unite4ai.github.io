# Literal review — `olas` (Olas Mech Marketplace)

Reviewer: LiteralReviewOlas, 2026-11-22. Read-only. Draft: `apps/web/src/content/exchange-profiles/olas.json`.
Sources opened for context: valory.xyz/mech-terms.pdf, olas.network/{disclaimer, faq, about, data, mech-marketplace, agent-economies/mech, olas-token, blog/q2-2026, blog/registries-update, blog/cheaper-faster-off-chain-requests-mech-marketplace}, stack.olas.network/mech-client/, the autonolas-marketplace README and SECURITY policy, docs/Vulnerabilities_marketplace.md and docs/configuration.json, etherscan (proxy, implementation, balance tracker), and marketplace.olas.network (HTML, app bundle, response headers and certificate).

Two facts the draft did not account for:
- **The marketplace footer** (app bundle `_app-*.js`, rendered by JS) reads `© Olas DAO <year> • Disclaimer • DAO Constitution`. "Disclaimer" links to https://olas.network/disclaimer and "DAO Constitution" to the IPFS constitution. The marketplace frontend links no other legal document. I read this text from the bundle; a browser render would be needed to confirm how it displays.
- **The Valory Mech Terms do not govern the marketplace.** They state: "These Mech Terms do not govern the Olas Marketplace itself; any Mech operated by anyone other than Valory; the Olas Protocol; the Olas DAO; or the OLAS token". They are Valory AG's terms for Valory Mechs, and section 2.2 says Valory does not operate the Marketplace.
