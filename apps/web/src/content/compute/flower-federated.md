Flower coordinates decentralized training rounds across clients that never share raw data.
It is the reference stack for healthcare and privacy-sensitive domains in the Unite4AI registry.

## When to choose federated compute

- Data cannot leave the institution (HIPAA, GDPR, community consent)
- You need open protocols, not a vendor-managed federation
- Multiple sites want to collaborate on the same model architecture
