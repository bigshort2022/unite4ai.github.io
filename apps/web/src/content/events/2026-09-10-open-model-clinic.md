No lecture. You bring a model you'd like to see in the registry, we work through the card together,
and you leave with a live entry and your name on the contributors page.

## Who this is for

Anyone who has never opened a pull request, and anyone who has opened hundreds. Both learn things
in this room — usually from each other.

## What to bring

- A model you built, fine-tuned, or use regularly
- Its license file (the actual file, not the README badge)
- A URL where its weights live

## Format

- **0:00–0:15** — What makes a card trustworthy, with two real examples: one excellent, one open-washed
- **0:15–1:00** — Work time, maintainers circulating
- **1:00–1:30** — Live review of volunteer submissions, then merges
