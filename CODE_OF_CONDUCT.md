# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Unite4AI exists because AI's benefits are distributed unevenly. A community that reproduced that
unevenness internally would have no standing to argue against it externally.

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or where in the world you are working from.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and ask before you accuse.
- Beginner questions are the point, not an interruption.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This card is missing its limitations section" — not
  "you clearly didn't think about this."
- Accept that people work across many time zones, languages, and levels of connectivity.
- Give credit generously and specifically.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexual attention or imagery, doxxing,
sustained disruption, and encouraging any of the above.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting a steward directly.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses, escalating by severity: a private correction; a public warning; temporary suspension;
permanent removal. Every enforcement action taken above a private correction is recorded in the
public decision log, with reporter identity withheld.

Maintainers who violate this code face the same process, applied by the stewards.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — repository, discussions, events, and public spaces where someone
is representing the project.
